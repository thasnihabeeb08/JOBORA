About Jobora

India's AI-powered job platform connecting students, freshers, and professionals with career opportunities.

Our Journey

Jobora was created to solve a fundamental problem in the Indian job market: the gap between talented individuals and the right career opportunities. As students ourselves, we understood the challenges faced by freshers and young professionals in finding meaningful work.

Our platform leverages cutting-edge AI technology to match candidates with opportunities based on skills, aspirations, and cultural fit, not just traditional qualifications.

Based in Kochi, Kerala, we're committed to serving job seekers across India with a focus on transparency, efficiency, and user-friendly experience.

Our Mission

To democratize job opportunities in India by making career growth accessible to students, freshers, and professionals through intelligent technology.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted career platform where everyone can find opportunities that match their skills and aspirations.
